About this patch

Strider EX brings the PC Engine CD version’s opening, mission briefings, boss dialogue and extended ending to the arcade game, alongside its additional desert stage. Cutscenes and boss portraits retain their original pixel width.

USA and Japanese builds include a hidden stage select, revised stage transitions and corrections to desert ledges, poles and camera movement. The USA build includes English boss voices through the original arcade sound hardware, with a new native FM score during the cinematics.

On MiSTer, the CPS+ Arrange launchers add the PC Engine soundtrack and voiced cinematics, with English and Japanese voice options and new full-quality English boss recordings. The required Strider EX CPS+ core is included.

Supply your own arcade ROMs, PC Engine CD, System Card BIOS and X68000 game disks. It runs in HBMAME and on MiSTer; stock MAME is used only to reconstruct the source scenes. This is a release candidate.

What's changed

  • PC Engine CD opening, mission briefings and extended ending, with English or Japanese captions
  • The extra Kyzylkum Desert stage, including its enemies and bosses
  • Boss dialogue scenes with native-size portraits and English or Japanese text
  • A Strider EX title and hidden stage select: hold attack and jump while pressing Start on the credited title screen
  • USA and Japanese arcade builds
  • A new native FM cinematic score and embedded English boss voices in the USA build
  • CPS+ arranged soundtrack and voiced cinematics on MiSTer, with English and Japanese voice options
  • Revised stage-clear transitions, desert ledge and pole contacts, and camera movement
  • Longer reading time for cinematic captions and a pause after high-score initials

Download

Tools updated 2026-10-27. Game version unchanged.

Reconstruction kit, rebuilds from your own disc + romset
strider-ex-rc1-kit.zip (21.9 MB)
SHA-256: fb380a91a94d3662b4aedd0c63fc9464b7de76fbf8a2311ef818087d40fbe5b4

The kit is our reconstruction code and a short list of byte patches. Everything else is rebuilt on your machine from files you already own.

What you supply

  • your own canonical MAME USA (striderua) and Japan (striderjr) arcade ROMs (ZIP, 7z or loose files); split sets also need the shared files in their strider parent archive
  • your own Strider Hiryuu (Japan) PC Engine CD as a complete Redump split-bin CUE rip or ZIP/7z archive
  • your own unheadered Japanese Super CD-ROM System v3.0 BIOS
  • your own Strider Hiryuu X68000 disk archive, used to restore the Aerial Battleship intro and ending music
  • Python 3.11 or later with NumPy, Pillow and SciPy; MAME 0.288 with Lua; 7-Zip for 7z inputs

How to build

unzip strider-ex-rc1-kit.zip -d strider-ex
cd strider-ex
python3 apply.py --disc /path/to/strider-pce.cue --romset /path/to/roms --bios /path/to/system-card.pce --x68k /path/to/strider-x68000.zip --out-dir out
