About this patch

Reconstruction requires your own Alpha 3 / Zero 3 arcade ROMs, the USA Alpha Anthology and Japanese Zero Fighter’s Generation PS2 disc images, and the Zero 3 Upper NAOMI GD-ROM and PIC. MiSTer play requires the CPS+ core and 128 MB SDRAM.

Street Fighter Alpha 3 Upper comes to CPS-2 with Guile, Fei Long, Dee Jay, T. Hawk, Evil Ryu and Shin Akuma. The boxer, Juli and Juni have their own cells on the select screen.

Guile, Fei Long, Dee Jay and T. Hawk bring their stages, music and arcade endings. The USA build includes the Street Fighter Alpha 3 English restoration; the Japan build keeps Japanese text and names.

This version includes Arcade mode and its two-player challenge. World Tour, Dramatic Battle and the other console modes are not included.

On MiSTer, it requires the included CPS+ core and 128 MB SDRAM. Computer play requires an HBMAME or FBNeo build that includes the Upper sets. This is a release candidate.

What's changed

  • Guile, Fei Long, Dee Jay, T. Hawk, Evil Ryu and Shin Akuma join the playable roster
  • The boxer, Juli and Juni have their own select cells
  • Added stages, arcade endings, voices and QSound music
  • USA (English) and Japan versions

Download

Reconstruction kit, rebuilds from your own disc + romset
sfa3-upper-cps2-rc1-kit.zip (4.9 MB)
SHA-256: 081351905d214805c518deacfe2e7ecfc730432561346ac3ed16a4ce9bc131a4

The kit is our reconstruction code and a short list of byte patches. Everything else is rebuilt on your machine from files you already own.

What you supply

  • Your own arcade ROM sets sfa3, sfa3u, sfz3j and qsound in canonical MAME format.
  • Your own USA Street Fighter Alpha Anthology and Japanese Street Fighter Zero: Fighter’s Generation PS2 disc images.
  • Your own Street Fighter Zero 3 Upper NAOMI GD-ROM CHD and sfz3ugd.zip PIC archive.
  • The Arcade Patches builder app prepares Python and its dependencies. To build by hand: Python 3.12 or newer, NumPy 2.2.6, SciPy 1.15.3 and chdman.

How to build

unzip sfa3-upper-cps2-rc1-kit.zip -d sfa3-upper-cps2
cd sfa3-upper-cps2
python3 apply.py --rompath /path/to/roms --ps2-us /path/to/us.chd --ps2-jp /path/to/jp.chd --naomi-chd /path/to/gdl-0002.chd --naomi-pic /path/to/sfz3ugd.zip --chdman /path/to/chdman --work /external/fresh-work --out-dir /external/fresh-output
