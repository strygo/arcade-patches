About

On MiSTer these need a 128 MB SDRAM module and the CPS+ core, which the kit includes. The MAME sets run in unmodified MAME using its Hyper Street Fighter II loader. HBMAME needs the set definitions the kit writes compiled in. About CPS-2+ hardware

Ultra Street Fighter II: The Final Challengers is the 2017 Nintendo Switch edition of Super Street Fighter II Turbo, and it never had an arcade release. This backport rebuilds it as a CPS-2 game.

Akuma has his own select cell, and Evil Ryu and Violent Ken join the original sixteen fighters with their Ultra moves, colors and endings. Shin Akuma stays hidden, for twenty fighters in all.

Ultra's presentation comes with them: the Ultra title and intro, "The Final Challengers" lettering, a color select for the new fighters, and a choice of sixteen stages or Random once you have picked a fighter.

The Switch edition's sound effects play in every build, and on MiSTer its arranged music plays too. Choose MODERN or CLASSIC sound effects in ULTRA CONFIGURATION; on MiSTer you can choose the music there too. MODERN controls are the default; choose CLASSIC in ULTRA CONFIGURATION to retain the earlier command inputs.

The USA build is in English; the Japan build keeps the Japanese.

This is a release candidate.

What's included

  • Akuma on his own select cell, with Evil Ryu and Violent Ken: twenty fighters with the hidden Shin Akuma
  • Ultra's classic colors for the new fighters, chosen on a color select screen
  • Sixteen stages and Random to choose from after you pick a fighter
  • The Ultra title and intro, and ULTRA on the VS, Continue and Game Over screens
  • The Switch edition's sound effects, and its arranged music on MiSTer; MODERN or CLASSIC options
  • MODERN command inputs with more forgiving motions, or CLASSIC inputs, selected in ULTRA CONFIGURATION
  • USA (English) and Japan builds

How it's distributed

Each build is put together on your own machine from files you already own. You need:

  • your own Nintendo Switch base game files: a USA NSP or XCI for the USA set, or a Japanese NSP for the Japan set
  • your own Switch prod.keys
  • your own arcade romset ssf2xj (with its parent ssf2t if yours is a split set) and the QSound firmware (qsound)
  • Python 3.10 or newer; extraction and audio tools download automatically

A small tool opens your Switch game files with your own keys, combines them with your arcade romsets, and writes out the finished MAME and HBMAME sets, the MiSTer files, and the music pack.

Download

Tools updated 2026-10-29. Game version unchanged.

Merged, split and complete archives and extracted ROM folders are accepted. Use --rompath /path/to/roms (repeatable) and --check to verify arcade inputs before rebuilding from your disc. 7z needs installed 7-Zip; ZIP does not.

Reconstruction kit, rebuilds from your own disc + romset
usf2-backport-rc5-kit.zip (20.6 MB)
SHA-256: 52e4e883fe889cab0ac5f3539fe7f0894552ea60665ab8c131b14fb082309d48

The kit is our reconstruction code and a short list of byte patches. Everything else is rebuilt on your machine from files you already own.

What you supply

  • your own Nintendo Switch base game files: a USA NSP or XCI for the USA set, or a Japanese NSP for the Japan set
  • your own Switch prod.keys
  • your own arcade romset ssf2xj (with its parent ssf2t if yours is a split set) and the QSound firmware (qsound)
  • Python 3.10 or newer; extraction and audio tools download automatically

How to build

unzip usf2-backport-rc5-kit.zip -d usf2-backport
cd usf2-backport
python3 apply.py --rompath roms --source-us usa.nsp --keys prod.keys --platform mame

Notes

  • MAME: RC4 replaces the previous default 4 MB, Modern-only audio build with an 8 MB audio build containing both Modern and Classic sound effects. It still runs in unmodified MAME as hsf2 (USA) or hsf2j (Japan); no custom MAME build is needed. Choose MODERN or CLASSIC in ULTRA CONFIGURATION. Use the newly generated ZIPs and keep them separate from original Hyper Street Fighter II ROMs. Checksum warnings are expected. HBMAME still needs the included set definitions compiled in.
  • Installation: install Python 3.10 or newer, extract the kit and run apply.py with your arcade ROMs, Switch game files and prod.keys. The region follows the game files you supply. Scratch and output folders are selected automatically unless you specify them. The installer checks paths before downloading tools, reports setup errors without a traceback, and groups the finished files and instructions by target. No compiler, Git, CMake, NumPy or separate FFmpeg installation is needed. For offline setup, keep cpsplus-tools-v2-offline.zip zipped and add --tools-bundle cpsplus-tools-v2-offline.zip --offline. Optional 7z inputs still need 7-Zip.
