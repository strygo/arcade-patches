Arcade Patches
Translation and restoration patches for arcade fighting games.

Fan-made patches for arcade games, mostly Japan-only CPS-2 and NAOMI releases. Everything is distributed as a patch against your own dump of the original game — binary diffs (IPS), MiSTer MRA overlays, or data-free GD-ROM patches; no game data is hosted here.

Everything on this site was built and verified against real MAME sets, with automated acceptance runs and human review of every change.

Night Warriors 2 Release candidate

Vampire Hunter 2 — English translation · Vampire Hunter 2: Darkstalkers Revenge (Japan 970929)

A complete English translation of the Japan-only Vampire Hunter 2, presented as the Night Warriors 2 that never shipped outside Japan.

Street Fighter Alpha 3 Upper

NAOMI GD-ROM multi-region patch · Street Fighter Zero 3 Upper (Japan, GDL-0002)

Removes the Japan-only region lock from the Zero 3 Upper GD-ROM so it boots on Japanese, USA, and Export NAOMI systems. One byte in the game header, properly re-encrypted.

Vampire Savior 2 Release candidate

English translation · Vampire Savior 2: The Lord of Vampire (Japan 970913)

English translation of the Japan-only Vampire Savior 2: Western character names, all 241 post-fight quotes, and every ending and final-boss dialog page.
