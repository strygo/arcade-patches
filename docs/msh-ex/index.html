About this patch

Marvel Super Heroes EX adds a free-play Console mode alongside the original Arcade mode. Choose from 13 fighters in both editions. The USA edition includes the full English Restoration.

What's changed

  • Open Versus, Training and Options from the Console mode title screen. Play Versus against a friend or the computer with handicap and stage selection.
  • Select all 13 fighters directly, including Doctor Doom, Thanos and Anita.
  • Choose from four colors for every fighter, including two extra colors.
  • Play the full English Restoration in the USA edition, including retranslated victory quotes, continue prompts, Automode tips and endings. The Japan edition retains the original Japanese text.
  • Practice without a time limit and adjust the dummy's behavior, guard, gauges, speed and Gems.
  • Choose Thanos's ending after clearing Arcade as Thanos.

The kit is our reconstruction code and a short list of byte patches. Everything else is rebuilt on your machine from files you already own.

What you supply

    Build or update

    Extract the kit, open a terminal in its folder, and run:

    python3 apply.py

    On Windows, use py -3.13 apply.py. Choose Build or update, then select your media folders and available outputs. Your media can stay where it is. The kit remembers your choices and prepares required helpers and Python packages privately on supported systems. Read INSTALL.txt in the completed output for the files to copy. Run the same command again to update.

    Advanced command-line reference

    How to build

    unzip msh-ex-rc1-kit.zip -d msh-ex
cd msh-ex
python3 apply.py --help
