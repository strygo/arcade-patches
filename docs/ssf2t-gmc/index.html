About this patch

Super Turbo came in two versions. Japan got Super Street Fighter II X, the one competitive players swear by. The rest of the world got a slightly different game: it plays differently, the continue screen is bare, most of the win quotes are gone, the endings are shorter, and Akuma's story is missing. Japan's version, of course, was in Japanese.

Grand Master Challenge is Japan's game in English. The fighting is untouched. Everything you read is new: all 136 win quotes, the strategy tips on the continue screen, the full endings, and Akuma's dialogue, translated fresh from the Japanese with Capcom's own English as the style guide.

It runs anywhere Super Turbo does: MAME, HBMAME, MiSTer, or a real CPS-2 board. The patch keeps the ROM's original sizes and key, so it drops in over the stock Japanese set.

What's changed

  • Japan's game, exactly as it plays in Japan
  • The Grand Master Challenge title, and a US region
  • Balrog, Vega, M. Bison and Akuma, the names you know
  • All 136 win quotes in English
  • The continue screen's strategy tips in English
  • Every ending in full, in English, including Akuma's
  • Akuma's intro dialogue, cut from the export game

How to apply

unzip ssf2t-gmc-rc2-ips.zip -d ssf2xj-patch
cd ssf2xj-patch
python3 apply.py /path/to/ssf2xj.zip --out-dir out

The script checks every file against the checksums below, applies the patches, checks the result, and writes the files for every platform in one go:

out/mame/ssf2xj.zip
out/hbmame/ssf2tgmc.zip
out/mister/_Arcade/_Arcade Patches/_Restorations/Super Street Fighter II Turbo Grand Master Challenge (USA) [English Restoration].mra
out/mister/games/hbmame/ssf2tgmc.zip

MAME and original hardware. Put the zip from out/mame/ ahead of the stock set in your MAME rompath (it keeps the name ssf2xj.zip), or burn its ROM files. MAME reports checksum warnings for the patched ROMs. That's expected, and the game runs normally.

HBMAME. Put the zip from out/hbmame/ in HBMAME's roms/ folder next to your stock ssf2xj.zip. It loads with no checksum warnings. The HBMAME set definition (ssf2tgmc) is generated with the restoration; an upstream submission is pending, so for now you add it to your own HBMAME build.

MiSTer. Copy the _Arcade folder from out/mister/ to the root of your SD card, and keep the stock, unmodified romset at games/mame/ssf2xj.zip (split MAME sets also need qsound.zip). You need Jotego's jtcps2 core, which update_all installs. The MRA applies the restoration in memory as the game loads, so nothing on your card is modified, and it keeps its own settings and saves under ssf2tgmc. The MiSTer-only download holds the same MRAs, ready to copy.

out/mister/ also holds the HBMAME set in games/hbmame/. The MRA above doesn't need it, but the CPS+ Arrange and HD Remix MRAs load it, so if you use CPS+, copy the games folder from out/mister/ to your card as well.

Any IPS patcher. Apply each file in ips/ (Flips, Lunar IPS, …) to the ROM file of the same name and re-zip the set yourself.

Release history

rc2 (2026-09-17)

  • The English was checked line by line against Capcom's own US script. Wherever Capcom's wording kept the Japanese meaning, it's now used: 54 lines across the win quotes, the endings and the Tournament Battle rules. Chun-Li's "Men are no challenge at all", for one, is now "You're no challenge at all". The Japanese story points stay, like Shoryuken instead of Sheng Long, and Cammy's spy story.
  • Every ending now plays out exactly as it does in Japan. Some scenes were being skipped under the US region, like Jane and Amy arriving to stop Guile.
  • Ending text looks like the US game's again: the fighters' lines in blue, everyone else in pink, and every caption centered in its box.
  • Akuma's reply to M. Bison is complete: "I am your shadow. Those possessed by the shadow must die. Your time is up!"
  • Dhalsim's teleport tip now explains that your input decides where he reappears.
  • Only sfxj.03d changes, so rebuild your set with the rc2 kit.

rc1 (2026-09-10)

  • First public release.
